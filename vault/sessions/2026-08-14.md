# Session — 14 Aug 2026

## The big one: the measurement laptop died

The i3-1115G4 failed. Its SSD was moved into the **Ryzen 9 270 (8C/16T)** and
now dual-boots there. That machine is now everything: Windows + WSL2 on one
side, Ubuntu on the other.

Consequences worked through and written down:

- `results/PROVENANCE.md` created — which datasets came from which machine,
  why they are not being re-taken, and what a Ryzen run would be *for*.
- `scripts/lib.sh:check_machine()` added and tested against the real
  mismatch: refuses to write a canonical dataset filename on a non-i3 CPU
  unless `OUT_SUFFIX` is set. Wired into `sweep.sh`, `fanout.sh`,
  `hugepage_ab.sh`.
- [[claims/machine]] and `CLAUDE.md` updated.

**The claims stay anchored to the i3.** A dead laptop does not change which
platform the claims should be *about* — same reasoning as
[[decisions/no-ryzen-n8-run]], decided *before* the failure, which is why it
does not read as rationalisation.

Nice inversion worth remembering: a Ryzen sweep is now a **better** experiment
than the N=8 run we cancelled. "Do the conclusions survive Zen vs Tiger Lake,
8 cores vs 2?" is a real reproducibility question. "Does N=8 look nicer" never
was. Still comes after form text and video.

## Layer 3 chosen and designed

User picked Layer 3 over the form text when offered the choice.
`docs/LAYER3_DESIGN.md` written: threat model, architecture, four phases, and
a **day-3 decision gate**. Summarised into [[decisions/layer3-is-the-novelty]]
and STATUS.md.

Prereq check paid for itself immediately — the old i3 had Secure Boot on and
**KVM unavailable**. Decided everything Layer 3 happens in a VM, module never
loaded on bare metal, with the argument for why nothing in Layer 3 needs bare
metal written into §8. On the Ryzen, WSL2 works ⇒ SVM is on in firmware ⇒ KVM
should need at most `modprobe kvm_amd`.
